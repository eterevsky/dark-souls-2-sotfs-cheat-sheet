Dark Souls 2: SotFS Checklist

Collection checklists for Dark Souls 2: Scholar of the First Sin — bosses, NPC questlines, rings, weapons, armour, spells and the rest — so you can tick things off as you go and see what a playthrough still owes you. Items are grouped by the area you first find them in. Progress is saved in your browser.

Forked from Xenevel's Dark Souls 2: SotFS Cheat Sheet, itself built on Stephen McNabb's original Dark Souls 2 Cheat Sheet. The step-by-step walkthrough has been dropped; what is left are the collection checklists. Full credits are at the bottom of the page.

Questlines

  • Mild-Mannered Pate - White Sign Soapstone, Pate's Set, Pate's Spear, Ring of Thorns
    • Forest of Fallen Giants - Talk to Pate by the trapped door near Cardinal Tower and exhaust his dialogue for the White Sign Soapstone
    • Forest of Fallen Giants - Summon him for The Last Giant and keep him alive; killing him at any point ends the quest and leaves only a gravestone
    • Earthen Peak - Exhaust his dialogue on the platform past the Manikins for Pate's Set, Pate's Spear and the Ring of Thorns
    • Brightstone Cove Tseldora - He and Creighton fight it out in the house below the Chapel Threshold cliff (running jump down to the doorway). Side with Pate for the Tseldora Den Key, or with Creighton for his set - you cannot have both
  • Creighton the Wanderer - Fist Pump gesture, Creighton's Set, Tseldora Den Key
    • Huntsman's Copse - Take the Undead Lockaway Key from the ledge, open the hut with the Undead Lockaway bonfire and exhaust his dialogue for the Fist Pump gesture
    • Huntsman's Copse - Rest at the bonfire so that he moves on
    • Shaded Woods - Talk to him at the top of the left-hand stone tower, out past the Shaded Ruins bonfire
    • Brightstone Cove Tseldora - Help him kill Pate, then exhaust his dialogue for the Tseldora Den Key and Creighton's Set
    • Brightstone Cove Tseldora - If Pate is already dead, hand over a plain Ring of Thorns instead (a +1 or +2 will not do); refusing turns Creighton hostile
  • Lucatiel of Mirrah - Lucatiel's Set, Mirrah Greatsword
    • No-Man's Wharf - Talk to her upstairs in the house between the two huts, then summon her at the pier before the Flexile Sentry's ship
    • The Lost Bastille - Talk to her behind the crates near The Tower Apart bonfire; her Lost Sinner sign waits at the bottom of the Sinner's Rise lift
    • Earthen Peak - Talk to her in the room facing the Pharros' Contraption
    • Black Gulch - Talk to her in the cave off the ledge before the tar pits, then summon her for The Rotten
    • Keep her alive through three of her boss fights - signs appear for the Flexile Sentry, the Lost Sinner, the Smelter Demon, The Rotten and the Burnt Ivory King. A fight she dies in does not count
    • Aldia's Keep - Once all three are done, exhaust her dialogue by the Foregarden bonfire for Lucatiel's Set and the Mirrah Greatsword. Leave that conversation alone until then
  • Benhart of Jugo - Joy gesture, Bluemoon Greatsword, Benhart's Set, Benhart's Parma
    • Majula - Talk to Benhart where he waits by the road to the Shaded Woods and exhaust his dialogue for the Joy gesture
    • Keep him alive through three of his boss fights - signs appear for Prowling Magus & Congregation, the Looking Glass Knight, the Giant Lord, Elana and Nashandra. Throne Watcher & Defender does not count, and a fight he dies in ends the questline
    • Shaded Woods - Talk to him again once Rosabeth is unpetrified and the road is open
    • Drangleic Castle - Talk to him by the Central Castle Drangleic bonfire, after the Dragonriders
    • Forest of Fallen Giants - Talk to him where he sits by the fallen giant past the Pursuer's arena; this opens the Memory of Orro
    • Memory of Orro - Exhaust his dialogue to inherit the Bluemoon Greatsword, Benhart's Parma and his set
  • Royal Sorcerer Navlaan - Chaos Set, Dispelling Ring, Simpleton's Spice, Forbidden Sun, Unleash Magic
    • Aldia's Keep - Speak to Navlaan through the barrier while Hollow; while human he only tells you to keep away. Never pull the lever that frees him - that voids the questline and turns him into a repeat invader
    • Aldia's Keep - He hands out four assassination contracts, each settled by bringing back proof. Every proof can be obtained without killing the target
    • Bring the Ladder Miniature, bought from Laddersmith Gilligan, for the Dispelling Ring
    • Bring Cale's Helm, from finishing Cale's questline, for Simpleton's Spice
    • Bring the Sunset Staff, which Felkin the Outcast gifts at 20 Intelligence and 20 Faith, for Forbidden Sun
    • Bring the Aged Feather, given by the Emerald Herald at Dragon Aerie, for Unleash Magic
    • Aldia's Keep - Settle all four contracts for the Chaos Set; he then stays on as a merchant
  • Head of Vengarl - Decapitate gesture, Vengarl's Set
    • Shaded Woods - Find the talking Head in the rubble in the foggy woods and exhaust his dialogue for the Decapitate gesture and Vengarl's Helm; he also sells items
    • Shaded Woods - Kill the Body of Vengarl, behind the door blocked by a petrified Lion Clan Warrior, for the rest of the set
  • Manscorpion Tark - Warmup gesture, Fragrant Branch of Yore, Second Dragon Ring, Black Scorpion Stinger
    • Shaded Woods - Equip the Ring of Whispers to talk to Tark in the sandy Shaded Ruins; without it he just tells you to leave
    • Shaded Woods - Summon him at the sign outside Scorpioness Najka's fog gate, which only appears once you have spoken to him
    • Shaded Woods - Return after Najka with the Ring of Whispers on for the Warmup gesture and a Fragrant Branch of Yore
    • Shaded Woods - Return once more after The Duke's Dear Freja for the Second Dragon Ring and the Black Scorpion Stinger
  • Weaponsmith Ornifex - Boss-soul weapon trades
    • Shaded Woods - Unpetrify the Lion Clan Warrior near the Shaded Ruins bonfire with a Fragrant Branch of Yore and take the Fang Key
    • Shaded Woods - Clear the Basilisks out of her cave before you unlock the door; they can kill her, and if she dies here her shop never opens
    • Shaded Woods - Free her with the Fang Key and exhaust her dialogue so she moves on
    • Brightstone Cove Tseldora - Find her in the statue room beside the Lower Brightstone Cove bonfire, where she trades boss souls for unique weapons
  • Grave Warden Agdayne - Have Mercy! gesture, Agdayne's shop, Darkdrift, Agdayne's Set
    • Undead Crypt - Approach Agdayne carrying no light at all; a lit torch or Cast Light makes him permanently hostile
    • Undead Crypt - Exhaust his dialogue for the Have Mercy! gesture and to open his shop
    • Undead Crypt - Come back once you hold the King's Ring for Darkdrift and Agdayne's Set
  • Darkdiver Grandahl - Pilgrims of Dark covenant, Resonant Soul, Great Resonant Soul, Climax, Xanthous Set, Dragon Chime
    • Shaded Woods - Talk to Grandahl below the hidden plank-covered hole near the stone towers
    • Black Gulch - Talk to him behind the great stone door, which needs the Forgotten Key
    • Drangleic Castle - Talk to him in the cavern by the Under Castle Drangleic bonfire, then join the Pilgrims of Dark
    • Hand him a Human Effigy each time you want to be sent into the Dark Chasm of Old
    • Dark Chasm of Old - Clear one chasm for rank 1 and Resonant Soul, then all three for rank 2 and Great Resonant Soul
    • Dark Chasm of Old - Clearing all three unlocks the Darklurker; kill it for rank 3 - Climax, the Xanthous Set and the Dragon Chime
  • Laddersmith Gilligan - Prostration gesture, ladders, Melu Scimitar, Ladder Miniature
    • Earthen Peak - Break the wooden railings by the Central Earthen Peak bonfire to reach Gilligan and exhaust his dialogue; his prices are 20% cheaper here than later
    • Majula - He moves to the edge of the Pit in front of the mansion once Mytha is dead
    • Spend 2,000 souls on his ladders or goods for the Prostration gesture
    • Majula - Buy the long ladder into the Pit for 12,000 souls; he then hands over the Melu Scimitar and the Ladder Miniature
  • Rosabeth of Melfia - Prism Stone, pyromancies, Pyromancy Flame upgrades
    • Majula - Unpetrify Rosabeth with a Fragrant Branch of Yore on the road to the Shaded Woods; she gives a Prism Stone. Leave the lever beside her alone until she has gone, or what it releases can kill her
    • Majula - Give her any piece of armour or clothing for the Change of Clothes trophy
    • Majula - Rest, then exhaust her dialogue; she settles by the monument, selling pyromancies and upgrading the Pyromancy Flame
  • Cale the Cartographer - House Key, Cale's Set
    • Forest of Fallen Giants - Find Cale digging in the dirt in the caves (down the Cardinal Tower ladder, through a hole and up a root) and exhaust his dialogue for the House Key
    • Majula - Open the mansion with the House Key and clear out the basement; Cale moves in once The Last Giant is dead
    • Majula - Light every flame on the basement map by reaching the areas it tracks, then talk to Cale for Cale's Set
  • Lonesome Gavlan - Travelling merchant (buys junk), Gyrm Greataxe
    • No-Man's Wharf - Find Gavlan upstairs in the house past the Dark Stalkers and talk to him
    • Harvest Valley - Talk to him again in the side cavern below the poison pool, through the hole in the rock
    • Doors of Pharros - Talk to him in the round room past the Gyrm Warrior; this is his last stop, and his consumables go unlimited here
    • Spend 16,000 souls with him across the three stops for the Gyrm Greataxe. Killing him before Doors of Pharros strands his shop where he died

  • Earthen Peak - Reveals an illusory wall hiding the Poisonbite Ring and a Soul of a Proud Knight
  • Earthen Peak - Fills a pool with poison; once the windmill is burnt the same pool runs clean and cures poison
  • Iron Keep - Fills a pool with healing water that raises fire defence for a while

  • Doors of Pharros - Past the second bridge - opens a chamber with a Gyrm Warrior and a chest of 30 Magic Arrows
  • Doors of Pharros - Past the second bridge - opens a passage that skips the next bridge entirely
  • Doors of Pharros - Three lockstones past the third bridge open the large door to a Rusted Mastodon and a Soul of a Brave Warrior

  • Aldia's Keep - Lights up the long hallway

Estus Flask Shards

  • Graft an Estus Flask Shard to your flask at the Emerald Herald in Majula for one more use. Twelve exist and eleven reach the cap of 12 uses, so one can be spared.
  • Majula - Corpse inside the well; strike the stone to raise it
  • Majula - Corpse past the Skeleton in the mansion basement
  • Things Betwixt - At the bottom of the pit, before the coffin
  • Forest of Fallen Giants - On a body on the Pursuer's platform
  • No-Man's Wharf - Corpse past the petrified hollow in the passage, which needs a Fragrant Branch of Yore
  • The Lost Bastille - Metal chest behind the McDuff's Workshop bonfire
  • Huntsman's Copse - On a body by the lever that opens the shortcut to the boss gate
  • Shaded Woods - In the room opposite the Old Akelarre bonfire
  • Brightstone Cove Tseldora - In the room by the lower platforms, with the spell-casting priest
  • The Gutter - On a corpse by the knight at the foot of the ladder
  • Drangleic Castle - Room with two Desert Sorceresses, below the Alonne Captain
  • Shrine of Amana - On the walkway between the second and third bonfires

Sublime Bone Dust

  • Cast Sublime Bone Dust into the Far Fire in Majula to raise the HP each Estus use restores. Five exist and +5 is the cap, so every one of them counts.
  • No-Man's Wharf - Iron chest guarded by a Royal Guard, in the alcove on the left of the passage
  • Huntsman's Copse - Chest past the lowered bridge, reached by dropping through the hole in the roof on the right
  • Shaded Woods - Iron chest in the building you reach through the window from the Shaded Ruins bonfire
  • The Gutter - Iron chest just before the first Gutter bonfire
  • Drangleic Castle - Room to the left as you enter, through the alcove past the main stairs

Soul Vessels

  • Hand a Soul Vessel to Strowen in Things Betwixt to redistribute your levels. The Brume Tower chest holds two, so these nine pickups come to ten vessels.
  • Majula - Given by Saulden the Crestfallen Warrior once four NPCs have settled in Majula
  • Majula - In the mansion chest
  • The Lost Bastille - Wooden chest behind a Pharros' door
  • Drangleic Castle - Iron chest past the Central Castle bonfire, by the imprisoned Milfanito
  • Undead Crypt - Wooden chest on the platform in the lowerable-bridge room
  • Memory of Orro - On a corpse on the lowest level
  • Dragon's Sanctum - Iron chest near the start, in the room with two Sanctum Knights
  • Brume Tower - Two of them, in an iron chest behind an illusory wall between the first and second bonfires
  • Frozen Eleum Loyce - Frozen chest on the roof before the second bonfire

Titanite Slabs

  • A Titanite Slab is the only way to take a weapon, shield or piece of armour from +9 to +10. Eleven are guaranteed per playthrough, and more can be farmed, so none of them is missable.
  • Majula - Bought from Stone Trader Chloanne for 13,000 souls, once Velstadt is dead
  • Forest of Fallen Giants - Chest behind the wall a Pharros contraption opens, beneath the ballista room
  • The Lost Bastille - Gifted by Steady Hand McDuff once you have spent 14,000 souls on his reinforcement and repair work; he also drops one if killed
  • Belfry Luna - Rank 1 reward in the Bell Keepers covenant
  • Harvest Valley - In the poison mist near the two Banediggers, by the way into the Heirs of the Sun room (not in the base game)
  • Brightstone Cove Tseldora - Guaranteed drop from Prowling Magus & Congregation
  • Drangleic Castle - Wooden chest in the acid pool, at Bonfire Intensity 2 and above
  • Dragon's Sanctum - Chest in the room with the first two Sanctum Knights
  • Cave of the Dead - Guaranteed drop from Afflicted Graverobber, Ancient Soldier Varg & Cerah the Old Explorer
  • Brume Tower - Behind the boxes by the Life Ring +3 chest
  • Frozen Eleum Loyce - In one of the chests Alsanna, Silent Oracle unfreezes
  • Also farmable: Crystal Lizards in the later areas drop one guaranteed, and Stone Knights in King's Passage (5%), The Imperfect in Dragon's Sanctum (5%), Stone Soldiers in Drangleic Castle (3%) and Iron Warriors in Brume Tower (2%) can. The Bell Keepers hand out more for defending the belfries, and Dyna & Tillo sometimes trade one for a Petrified Something.
